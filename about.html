Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We're here to help you understand how fctCare operates.

01. How do I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

You can easily cancel or reschedule your appointment through the profile dashboard, or by contacting our 24/7 customer support line at least 2 hours before the scheduled time.

02. Is my online payment and medical data secure?

Yes, absolutely. fctCare uses bank-grade encryption technologies. Your personal information, payment details, and medical history are strictly confidential.

03. Will I get a refund if the doctor cancels the session?

Yes. If a doctor cancels a session, a full refund (including the channeling fee) will be automatically credited back to your bank account within 3-5 working days.
